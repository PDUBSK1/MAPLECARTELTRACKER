Maple Cartel V1 — quantity reading patch

Problem: the quickslot crop is often 16–20 pixels tall, while the game stack digits use a native ~12px font. The OCR previously selected a large-font reader based on overall crop height.

Fix: scan native-height subrows with the original small-font recognizer and require repeated matching observations. Preserve the original large-font fallback and EXP/level pipeline.

Safety: questionable or conflicting counts remain unreadable rather than charging false potion expenses.
