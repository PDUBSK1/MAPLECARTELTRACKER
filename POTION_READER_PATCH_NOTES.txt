Maple Cartel V1 - original quickslot recognizer diagnostic patch

Only vendor/maple-track/quickslot-ocr.js has been changed.
Adds exported diagnoseSlot(image) to inspect raw candidate guesses and rejection reasons.
Original recognizeSlot(image), EXP tracking, layout, and accepted-count rules are unchanged.

Confirmed on user-provided 64x24 samples:
- Actual 386: original reader rejects; best 3-digit raw candidate 306 (error 0.1903).
- Actual 420: original reader rejects; a raw 3-digit candidate is 490 (error 0.1987).
Both fail 0.08 error threshold. Loosening that threshold would accept wrong readings.

This is a diagnostic patch, NOT a functioning potion-recognition fix. Do not deploy it to replace a working website. No in-game tests have been performed.
